PRIVACY POLICY

01. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaivelu F&B Pvt Ltd ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with our website www.partay.in, mobile applications, social media pages, and related services ("Services").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle your personal data. This Policy applies to all visitors, users, and customers of our Services.

02. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number, mailing address)
  • Account registration details (username, password)
  • Purchase and transaction information (product orders, payment details)
  • Communication data (emails, support tickets, customer service inquiries)
  • User-generated content (Instagram posts, photos, comments for UGC campaigns)
  • Demographic information (age, location, preferences)
  • Feedback, reviews, and survey responses

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information (device type, operating system, browser type)
  • IP address and geolocation data
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies
  • Website usage data (pages visited, time spent, links clicked)
  • Analytics data through Google Analytics and similar tools
  • Social media interaction data (likes, shares, comments on Instagram and other platforms)

03. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data for Service Delivery (fulfilling product orders, customer support), Marketing and Communications (promotional emails, UGC campaigns), Business Operations (fraud detection, app performance), and Analytics.

04. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information based on consent, contract performance, legal obligations, and legitimate business interests. For residents of India: We comply with the Information Technology Rules, 2011, and Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023.

05. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We share data with trusted payment processors, shipping partners, and marketing services. We do NOT sell personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.

06. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to request a copy of your personal data, correct inaccurate details, request deletion, or unsubscribe from marketing at any time by contacting: privacy@partay.in.
